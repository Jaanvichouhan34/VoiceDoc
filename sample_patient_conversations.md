## 1. Patient: Rekha Chouhan (Acute Gastritis / Acid Reflux)
- **Age / Gender**: 42 Yrs / Female
- **Vitals**: BP: 128/82 mmHg | HR: 76 bpm | Temp: 98.4°F
- **Diagnosis**: Acute Gastritis & Hyperacidity

### 🎙️ Voice Transcript / Consultation Log:
> **Doctor**: Namaste Rekha ji, bataiye aaj kya takleef hai?
> 
> **Patient**: Namaste Doctor saab. Mujhe 3 din se pet ke oopri hisse mein bahut burning aur acidity ho rahi hai. Khana khane ke baad seene mein jalan hoti hai aur kabhi kabhi nausea bhi aata hai.
> 
> **Doctor**: Achha, khane mein spicy ya bahar ka khana khaya tha? Vomiting toh nahi hui?
> 
> **Patient**: Haan doctor, shaadi thi do din pehle toh thoda oily aur teekha khana ho gaya tha. Vomiting nahi hui, par khatti dakarein aati hain.
> 
> **Doctor**: Aapse sunkar lagta hai yeh Acute Gastritis aur Hyperacidity hai. BP 128/82 hai, bilkul normal. Aapko Pantoprazole 40mg subah khaali pet 7 din ke liye leni hai, aur Gelusil syrup khane ke baad 2 chammach. Teekha, oily khana aur chai-coffee band karein. 7 din baad milen.

## 2. Patient: Rajesh Kumar (Hypertension & Type 2 Diabetes Follow-up)
- **Age / Gender**: 54 Yrs / Male
- **Vitals**: BP: 142/90 mmHg | HR: 82 bpm | Temp: 98.6°F | RBS: 178 mg/dL
- **Diagnosis**: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus & Essential Hypertension

### 🎙️ Voice Transcript / Consultation Log:
> **Doctor**: Good morning Rajesh ji. Aapka regular follow-up hai aaj. Kaise hain aap?
> 
> **Patient**: Doctor saab, waise toh thik hu, par sham ko thodi thakan aur sar mein halka bhari-pan lagta hai. Fasting sugar check kiya tha kal, 140 aaya tha.
> 
> **Doctor**: Dekhiye, aaj aapka BP 142/90 aaya hai jo thoda high hai, aur Random sugar 178 mg/dL hai. Aap apni Telmisartan 40mg regular le rahe hain?
> 
> **Patient**: Haan doctor, tablet toh roz subah leta hu, par mithaas thodi zyada ho gayi thi tyohar par.
> 
> **Doctor**: Diabetes aur BP mein diet control bahut zaroori hai. Aapse Telmisartan 40mg continue rakhne ko bol raha hu subah, aur Metformin 500mg din mein do baar khane ke baad leni hai. Mithai aur namak kam karein, daily 30 minute walk karein. 1 month baad HbA1c testing ke saath aaiye.

## 3. Patient: Aarav Sharma (Viral Fever & Respiratory Infection)
- **Age / Gender**: 24 Yrs / Male
- **Vitals**: BP: 118/76 mmHg | HR: 88 bpm | Temp: 101.2°F
- **Diagnosis**: Acute Upper Respiratory Viral Infection

### 🎙️ Voice Transcript / Consultation Log:
> **Doctor**: Hello Aarav, kya pareshani ho rahi hai?
> 
> **Patient**: Doctor saab, do din se tez bukhar hai around 101 degree. Sath mein khasi, gale mein dard aur badan dard bhi hai.
> 
> **Doctor**: Kaph (sputum) aara hai ya sookhi khasi hai? Sans lene mein dikkat toh nahi?
> 
> **Patient**: Sookhi khasi hai doctor, aur raat ko bukhar badh jata hai. Sans lene mein koi dikkat nahi hai.
> 
> **Doctor**: Temperature abhi 101.2°F hai. Yeh Acute Upper Respiratory Viral Infection hai. Pareshan mat hoiye. Paracetamol 650mg din mein 3 baar 3 din ke liye lein. Combined Cough Syrup 10ml din mein do baar lein. Garam paani ke garare karein aur rest karein. 3 din mein bukhar kam ho jayega.

## 5. Patient: Vikram Singh (Acute Lumbar Strain / Lower Back Pain)
- **Age / Gender**: 48 Yrs / Male
- **Vitals**: BP: 134/86 mmHg | HR: 78 bpm | Temp: 98.2°F
- **Diagnosis**: Acute Lumbar Muscle Strain

### 🎙️ Voice Transcript / Consultation Log:
> **Doctor**: Namaste Vikram ji, aaiye baithiye. Kya takleef hai?
> 
> **Patient**: Doctor saab, kal gym mein heavy samaan uthaya tha, tabse kamar ke nichle hisse mein tez dard hai. Jhukne mein aur uthne-behtne mein bahut takleef ho rahi hai.
> 
> **Doctor**: Pairo mein koi suun-pan (numbness) ya tingling toh nahi aari?
> 
> **Patient**: Nahi doctor, bas kamar mein hi kadak-pan aur tez dard hai.
> 
> **Doctor**: Yeh Acute Lumbar Muscle Strain (Sprain) hai. Tension mat lein, nerves safe hain. Aceclofenac + Paracetamol + Thiocolchicoside tablet din mein do baar 5 din ke liye lein. Hot water bag se kamar ki sekai karein aur heavy lifting bilkul na karein. 5 din baad batayein.
